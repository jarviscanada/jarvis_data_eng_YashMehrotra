# Stock Return Modeling Executive Summary

## Decision

State whether to proceed, iterate, or stop. Give the main reason and specify whether the decision applies to research, paper trading, or live capital.

## Scope

Summarize the investment universe, prediction horizon, evaluation dates, models tested, portfolio rule, and transaction-cost assumption.

## Results

Report the selected strategy beside its main benchmarks. Include annualized return, volatility, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown, final portfolio value, turnover, and cost sensitivity.

## Interpretation

Explain what improved, what did not, and whether the result supports the decision. Separate statistical model performance from portfolio performance.

## Risks

Identify the most important data, validation, market, and execution limitations. Do not imply that historical performance establishes future profitability.

## Next Decision Gate

Define one measurable next step, its minimum evaluation period, pass or fail criteria, and the evidence required before the next deployment decision.
